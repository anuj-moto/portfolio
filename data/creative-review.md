# Creative Director Review

After QA passes, review the portfolio through these lenses. Be honest — fix what's weak.

## First Impression (0-3 seconds)
- Is the hero immediately communicating who this person is?
- Is the primary call-to-action obvious?
- Does the visual hierarchy draw the eye correctly?
- Would someone understand the person's domain without reading a word?

## Craft & Polish
- Any pixel-level misalignments?
- Is letter-spacing intentional on headings?
- Are all shadows consistent in direction and softness?
- Do all icons match in weight and style?
- Are gradients sophisticated or cheesy?
- Is whitespace used confidently, not just as leftover space?

## Motion & Interaction
- Does every animation serve a purpose, or is it decoration?
- Are animations fast enough to not feel sluggish?
- Is the signature interaction performant and not disorienting?
- Do hover states feel intentional and consistent?
- Are transitions smooth with proper easing curves?

## Emotional Resonance
- Does this feel like it belongs to a real, specific person — not a template?
- Would a creative director at a top studio pause and look?
- Is there one moment of genuine delight or surprise?
- Does the design match the stated vibe (bold, minimal, playful, etc.)?

## Content Quality (if using placeholders)
- Do project titles sound like real work?
- Does the bio read like a person wrote it, not an AI?
- Are placeholder images relevant to the domain?
- Do testimonials (if any) sound believable?
